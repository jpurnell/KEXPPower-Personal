# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Fixed
- CarPlay crashed the moment the car connected. The app had no CarPlay scene entry in its Info.plist, and the project's generated scene manifest was replacing any entry added; CarPlay raised an exception while handing over its interface controller. The iOS target now uses its own manifest naming `CarPlaySceneDelegate`. Found from the crash reports on the phone (four identical reports, `-[CPTemplateApplicationScene _deliverInterfaceControllerToDelegate]`); verified on the CarPlay simulator.
- The car showed "Retrieving…" until a song was started, because the model fetched the show only when a window asked and CarPlay can launch the app with none. The car now asks for the fetch itself on connect.
- The widget showed a blank grey square when a song had no cover (KEXP gives none for some plays) and a placeholder note when the cover could not be downloaded (archive.org answers some albums with an HTTP 500 error page). It now tries the cover, then the KEXP logo, and only then the note (`WidgetArtworkResolver`, tested: download failure, an error page that is not an image, a host off the allow-list, nothing usable). A downloaded error page is no longer mistaken for artwork.
- The widget's artwork keeps its colour when the system tints the widget (a Mac desktop widget behind other windows, an iPhone widget in accented mode); before, the logo was flattened to a blank light square. This is iOS 18 and macOS 15 behaviour (`widgetAccentedRenderingMode`), so it is not covered by an automated test: please check it on the desktop.

### Added
- Archive: browse by Date, Host, Show or Genre from a picker at the top. Host, Show and Genre are alphabetical cards laid out in an adaptive grid (one column on an iPhone, more on an iPad or Mac) with how many shows each has in the last two weeks (a Host card carries the host's photo, with a placeholder when KEXP has none or cannot be reached; a Show card carries its picture, hosts and genres); Genre lists whatever genres the shows carry, with a show counted under each of its genres, so a new genre appears without a code change; choosing one lists its shows newest first with the day and time. The groupings are made on the device because KEXP's API can filter shows by time but not by host, program or genre. Date mode: pick one of the last fifteen days from tiles (month, day number, weekday; today highlighted), see that day's shows in the order they aired with their host, time and picture, and tap one to hear it from its start in place of the live stream. A bar shows the archived show with a way back to live. The lock screen shows the show while it plays, and the live poll no longer overwrites it with the live song. A show whose audio is no longer kept, or a failed request, gives a notice instead of silence. The bar for an archived show is a player: a scrubber over the show (not the file, which starts before the show and overlaps its neighbours) with elapsed and remaining time, play and pause, back 15 seconds, forward 30 seconds, and a lightning-bolt button that catches up to the live stream; skipping or scrubbing past the end of the show goes on to the next one, or to live. The player stays over every section while a show plays (fold it down to its title line with the chevron), so playback can be controlled from Live or Review too; the Playlist button lists the show's songs with the one on now marked, and choosing one jumps to where it aired; the clock under the scrubber shows the time of day at the station that the scrubber is on, so "the thing at 12:15" can be found. Once the live stream has been started, a slim "KEXP Live" bar with play and pause stays beneath every other section, so live can be paused from Archive or Review too (the Live section keeps its own button). Times in the archive (the scrubber's clock, the playlist, and each show's start) are shown in the listener's time zone, with its abbreviation; the days themselves are still the station's, because KEXP's broadcast day begins at Seattle's midnight. Each song in the archive playlist has the same add-to-playlist button as on Live, and a song saved in one place shows as saved in the other, because they are the same KEXP play. KEXP's archive is sometimes damaged (the station's power outage on 2026-10-30; a Morning Show on 2026-11-02 whose file holds only its first twenty minutes). A file that does not belong to the show is now refused as "no longer available" instead of played, a recording shorter than its show carries a note saying how much was recorded, and songs past the end of the recording are dimmed in the playlist and say "Not recorded" instead of jumping on to the next show; a show with no playlist says "KEXP has no songs logged for it". Requires StationKitAdapterKEXP 0.7.0. While an archived show plays, the lock screen, Control Center, headphones and CarPlay's Now Playing screen offer skip back 15, skip forward 30 and a scrubber over the show (the same arithmetic as the on-screen player, so a scrub past the end rolls into the next show), and say it is a recording rather than a live stream; back on live they offer none of these. Requires StationKit 0.11.0. The commands are checked on a device, not by an automated test. The CarPlay Archive tab and, and the Mac and CarPlay layouts are later phases (`ArchiveBrowser.md`). When an archived show ends the player rolls into the next show, joined where the previous file ended (archive files overlap by about ten minutes), or onto the live stream when no ended show follows; the next item is lined up 30 seconds before the end. The player is now an `AVQueuePlayer`. A debug-only Probe button opens the playback test panel used on a device. Requires StationKit 0.11.0 and StationKitAdapterKEXP 0.7.0.
- Review section (iPhone, iPad, Vision Pro; not the Mac, which cannot add to playlists): a text-guess Apple Music match is held there instead of being guessed at. Each entry shows what KEXP played and the Apple Music suggestion, both with their covers saved as small image files, with Play (the 30-second Apple Music preview; pauses the radio and does not resume it), Add and Not this one. A count badge shows on the tab. From CarPlay a guess goes straight into the queue and the car says "Saved for review"; from the phone the prompt gains "Review later". Exact (ISRC) matches are still added at once. Queue, store, artwork store and preview model are tested (`ReviewQueueTests`, `UserDefaultsReviewStoreTests`, `ReviewPreviewModelTests`, `SongSavingReviewTests`). Proposal: `project/plans/proposals/ReviewQueue.md`. The Apple Music cover comes from StationKit 0.8.0 (`CatalogSong.artworkURL`, filled by `MusicKitCatalog`; the app now requires 0.8.0). **Not yet checked on a device:** that previews play, how they share audio with the radio, and Apple's image host (`mzstatic.com` is assumed).
- A persistent diagnostic log (`DiagnosticLog`, `Application Support/Diagnostics/spinner-log.txt`): each line is flushed to disk before the call returns, so it survives the crash it is meant to explain. CarPlay connection, screens, row taps and add-to-playlist are recorded. Read it from the phone with `xcrun devicectl device copy from --device <name> --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier com.JPEnterprises.Spinner --source "Library/Application Support/Diagnostics/spinner-log.txt" --destination <dir>`.
- CarPlay: a held Apple Music match shows a phone icon instead of a question mark, and a short notice names the song and says to review it on the phone (`CarPlayContent.saveNotice`). A review section is proposed in `project/plans/proposals/ReviewQueue.md`.
- CarPlay (iPhone app): the car shows a Live tab and an Archive tab. Live has two rows: the show on the air with its host (for example "The Midday Show" over "Cheryl Waters") and the song on now; choosing either starts the stream and opens the system's Now Playing screen, which carries an add-to-playlist button. The button adds the song to the same "KEXP" playlist as the phone's button, with the same record that stops a song being added twice, and shows its state (add, adding, added, not found). A match that is only a text guess is held, not added, and the car says \"Review on your phone\"; the phone's \"Is this the song?\" question waits there. Archive is a disabled row saying \"Coming soon\" until the archive is built. The car and the phone share one player and one saving model. Built from Apple's CarPlay templates; the words and button states (`CarPlayContent`) are tested; the screens themselves are not covered by an automated test and have not yet been checked in a CarPlay simulator or a car: please look at them.
- A watch widget for the Smart Stack and watch faces, built from the same StationKit types as the phone widget: `accessoryRectangular` (title, artist, "Last played 2:10 PM", and a play/pause button), `accessoryCircular` (a play/pause button), `accessoryInline` ("Title – Artist") and `accessoryCorner` (the button with the song along the bezel). The watch app now publishes what is on the air and the playing state the moment the player changes, through StationKit's `WidgetFeed`, and the widget's button runs in the watch app and tells the widget the state to expect before the stream has started. The watch uses the same App Group as the phone (`group.com.JPEnterprises.Spinner`); Xcode registered it for the two watch App IDs on first build, no portal step needed (checked: the group is in the signed binaries and in both provisioning profiles). The words the complications show (`AccessorySummary`) are tested. Checked on an Apple Watch Ultra 3 (2026-11-05): the song shows, and once playback has been started in the app a tap on the complication plays and pauses. A tap on a cold watch, before the app has ever started playback, did not start audio: a watch needs an audio route (headphones, or the connection the app asks for in the foreground), and a background tap cannot ask for one. The widget showed "playing" anyway until the next poll; now a failed start is published at once and the widget is corrected seven seconds after any tap, so it no longer claims playback that is not happening. Still open: making a tap on a cold watch open the app so the route can be chosen, and how often the watch actually refreshes the widget.
- The iOS app declares the CarPlay audio entitlement (`com.apple.developer.carplay-audio`; Apple approved it for this app on 2026-11-05). Verified in a signed build: the key is in the app's signature and in the provisioning profile. There is no CarPlay screen yet (`project/plans/proposals/CarPlayAudio.md`).
- A Control Center control, "KEXP" (iOS 18 and macOS 26; the SDK does not offer controls on visionOS): a toggle that plays or pauses the stream without opening the app and shows Playing or Paused, in Control Center, on the lock screen and on the Action button. It reads the same play state the widget does, and the app tells it the moment the player changes (the same publish-on-change path that fixed the widget's lagging icon). A control asks for a state, not a flip, so a stale control cannot invert what is playing (`ControlPlayState`, tested). Not yet checked in Control Center: please add it on Brawn (Control Center, Add a Control, KEXP).
- macOS: an "Open in Music" button on each song (MusicKit cannot add to a playlist on the Mac, so this opens the song in Music, where you add it). The song is looked up when you click, not for every row, and a miss shows a "not found" state and can be tried again. `OpenInMusicModel` has unit tests.
- macOS: rows take keyboard focus: Tab reaches a row and Return expands or collapses it. Space stays the Playback menu's play/pause. A row had been a click-only gesture since the click-area fix.
- A Mac UI test target, `Spinner Mac UITests`, that drives the real app against fixed songs (launch argument `-uitest-fixtures`, `UITestFixtures`: no network and no audio): a click at the top, middle and bottom of a Previously row expands it (the earlier top-half-only defect), Tab then Return expands the focused row, and every song has the Open in Music button. Running it needs UI automation enabled on the Mac (a one-time authentication).
- `scripts/bootstrap.sh`: enables the git hooks after a fresh clone (`core.hooksPath` is per-clone config and cannot be committed) and checks that `swift` and `quality-gate` are installed. `CLAUDE.md` already described it; the script did not exist, so a fresh clone silently ran no hooks. Safe to run twice (checked in a fresh clone).

- Groundwork for pausing and rewinding the live stream (`project/plans/proposals/LiveRewind.md`, Phase 0; nothing you can see yet): `ADTSSegmenter` cuts the live stream's raw AAC into short segments on frame boundaries, however the bytes arrive, and `LiveBuffer` keeps the last ten minutes in memory and writes the HLS playlist, with the timestamp tags a raw AAC segment needs and a marker for each break in the stream. 29 tests on synthetic frames, no network and no player. The local server, the recorder and the player come next and need a device to check.

### Changed
- Requires `StationKit` 0.11.0 and `StationKitAdapterKEXP` 0.7.0 (this started as `StationKit` 0.7.0 for the widget's publishing logic; the archive raised it). The widget's publishing logic (save on change, heartbeat the playing flag, reload only when something changed) and the play-state expectation window moved into StationKit as `WidgetFeed` and `PlayStateExpectation`, so the watch app can share them; `WidgetPublisher` now only turns KEXP's plays and shows into a snapshot and delegates. Behaviour is unchanged: its tests pass as before, and the tests of the moved logic now live in StationKit.

## 3.3.0 - 2026-11-05

The widget, add to playlist, the Mac mini player and sidebar, and a fix for the widget's play/pause icon. Still private.

### Fixed
- The widget's play/pause icon lagged and sometimes stayed wrong. The app published the playing state only on its five-second poll (measured: 3.5 to 5 seconds to flip after Play), and a poll that failed skipped the heartbeat, so the flag could go stale while playing. Now the state is published about 5 ms after the player changes and the widget is reloaded then; the widget's own button publishes the state it is about to cause before returning, so the reload WidgetKit does after a tap shows the right icon, and the poll does not undo it while the stream starts; a failed poll still refreshes the flag. A reload is still the system's to schedule: background reloads are budgeted, so a change can still show late. The app and the widget log each published change and each entry built (subsystem `com.JPEnterprises.Spinner` and `com.JPEnterprises.Spinner.widget`).

### Added
- A Now Playing widget in three sizes (iPhone and iPad home screen, Mac desktop, Vision Pro on visionOS 26): small is the art with the song and artist; medium is the now-playing row (art, time, title, artist, album, release date and label) with play/pause; large adds the show and host, Spotify and Bandcamp links and the start of the DJ's comment. It shows "Last played 3:36 PM", never "live", and asks to be refreshed every five minutes (the system decides how often it actually does). The app publishes the current song to an App Group and reloads the widget only when the song, the show or the play state changes, so it does not spend WidgetKit's refresh budget on every poll. Play/pause runs in the app without opening it. The app and the widget share data through an App Group, which differs by platform: `group.com.JPEnterprises.Spinner` on iOS and visionOS (registered in the portal) and a Team-ID-prefixed group on the Mac, where a `group.` identifier is refused without an explicit profile (found when the Mac app's writes to the group were refused). Verified: the Mac app writes the current song, show and host to its group. The widget on Brawn: the owner reported the build working after the play-state fix (2026-11-05). Not yet checked on the Mac desktop.
- Add to playlist (iPhone, iPad, Vision Pro): a button on each song in the list, and one on the "Now Playing" line (not on airbreaks, which are not songs; the Now Playing row keeps its full width). A tap adds the song to a "KEXP" playlist (made on first use); a press and hold also offers another of your playlists, or a new one, to add it to as well. Songs are matched by ISRC when KEXP supplies a MusicBrainz recording id (looked up on MusicBrainz, paced and cached), otherwise by a text search that asks "Is this the song?" before adding. A song is never added to the same playlist twice, including across launches. The button shows added, partly added, needs confirmation, not found, Apple Music unavailable, and needs permission. Needs the MusicKit service on the App ID and an Apple Music subscription.
- The iOS app declares the App Group `group.com.JPEnterprises.Spinner` (the widget will share data through it). That makes Xcode sign with a profile for the explicit App ID instead of the wildcard `*` profile, which grants no capabilities.
- When an add to a playlist fails, the log records MusicKit's own error and the account state (authorization, catalog playback, cloud library), because MusicKit's description of these failures is often just "An unknown error occurred".
- macOS has no add button: MusicKit marks adding to playlists and creating them unavailable on the Mac (checked in the macOS 27.2 SDK). The Mac can search the catalog and read the library; the plan's earlier note that it could write was wrong and is corrected.
- macOS: a mini player window, translucent (the desktop shows through, blurred; solid with Reduce Transparency; draggable by its background), with just what is playing (artwork, title, artist, play/pause and a button back to the full window). Playback menu: Show Mini Player (⇧⌘M) and Show Full Window (⌥⌘M). The main window is a single-instance window, so switching never opens a second one.
- `NowPlayingSummary`: the title and artist lines shown for a play ("Airbreak" and "KEXP" when missing), shared by the mini player and, later, the widget.

### Changed
- Requires `StationKit` 0.6.0 (the widget layer) and `StationKitAdapterKEXP` 0.4.0 (labels, release date and the current show), on top of the earlier requirements below.
- Requires `StationKit` 0.4.0 (song matching) and `StationKitAdapterKEXP` 0.3.0 (the mapping carries the MusicBrainz recording id), as well as
  `KEXPPowerKit` 4.1.0, which gives `Play` its MusicBrainz ids (`recordingID`, `releaseID`, `releaseGroupID`, `trackID`) for matching songs to Apple Music.
- The app has a frame: a sidebar (Live, Archive, Playlists) on the Mac, iPad and Vision Pro that can be shown and hidden (toolbar button, View menu), and tabs on the iPhone. Live is what the app showed before; Archive and Playlists say they are coming. `AppSection` drives both.
- Starting the playlist is idempotent (`startIfNeeded`), so the mini window can start it on its own and several windows can ask.

## 3.2.0 - 2026-11-04

First tagged version of the app (the number continues this repository's tag history, which ends at 3.1.1 from the library it was forked from; `0.1.0` was already taken by an old upstream tag): live KEXP playlist and stream on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro and Apple Watch, built on `StationKit`, `StationKitAdapterKEXP` and `KEXPPowerKit`. Private until it has been used for a while; the Apple Music playlist, widget and archive features are not built yet.

### Added
- A standalone watchOS app, `Spinner Watch` (`com.JPEnterprises.Spinner.watchkitapp`, watchOS 10): live KEXP stream on the 64 kbps option, now-playing and play/pause, lock-screen and remote-command integration through `StationKit`, long-form audio session. A fresh launch can take 35–40 s to reach a working connection on a cold watch network, so the app shows "Connecting…" until the stream is ready (a tap then means "play when ready"), retries track info after 3, 6, 9 s and so on up to 15 s, and offers "Tap to retry" if the stream fails. Embedded in the iOS app (iOS builds only; the macOS and visionOS builds of the same target leave it out). The button shows a spinner while the stream buffers and a tap then cancels; album art is shown. Background audio is declared with `WKBackgroundModes` (a build setting for the key is silently dropped on watchOS, which made audio fade out when the app left the foreground). The audio mode is also declared with `UIBackgroundModes`, which is what made background playback work on the Ultra. Player status, waiting reason, item errors and the audio route are logged (category `WatchPlayer` / the iOS player's category). Volume changes are logged. The Digital Crown sets the volume through an invisible system volume control, and a slim level bar shows along the right edge only while the volume is changing.

### Changed
- The player and playlist are owned by the app, not created inside `ContentView`, so every window and the menus share one player. New Playback menu (Space plays or pauses; its title follows the player). The Play/Pause wording lives in one place (`PlaybackMenuState`).
- macOS is its own target, `Spinner Mac` (`Spinner.app`, bundle ID `com.JPEnterprises.Spinner`, macOS 14, hardened runtime): it shares the app's source files and assets but has its own entitlements (sandbox and outgoing network only; the iOS file's `network.server` is not carried over), build settings and scheme. The iOS target now builds for iOS and visionOS only. The Mac app still has no playback; that is the next step.
- New app icon on every platform: four bars in a yellow gradient with the proportions of the KEXP logo (same heights, spacing, bottom-aligned), without the wordmark. The wave is its own transparent layer on visionOS; iOS and the App Store icon sit it on a dark warm background (opaque); macOS bakes the rounded tile into the canvas; the watch icon is the wave on near-black, sized to stay inside the circle the system masks it to.
- Player glue (Now Playing snapshot and publisher, audio session coordinator, artwork loader) now comes from `StationKit` 0.2.0, and the KEXP stream and now-playing mapping from `StationKitAdapterKEXP` 0.2.0; the app's copies and their tests (moved to StationKit) are removed. `KEXPPowerKit` is 4.0.1.
- visionOS: the app icon's last layer is opaque and the logo is its own transparent layer; the App Store icon has no alpha; the macOS icon fills the standard icon grid. `allowsExternalPlayback` is not used on visionOS, where it does not exist.
- The app consumes the KEXPPower library as a package (`jpurnell/KEXPPowerKit` 4.0.0, by URL; `Play` is now a `Sendable` struct) instead of
  compiling a fork's sources into the app target. See `project/decisions/ADR-002-library-in-its-own-repo.md`.
- Now Playing: a single publisher (`LegacyNowPlayingPublisher`) writes system state; the player no longer
  does. `PlayerController.isPlaying` mirrors the player's own `timeControlStatus`, so remote commands and
  interruptions cannot leave it out of step.
- The playlist refresh requests 50 plays instead of 2,000 every five seconds, and a refresh can no longer
  start a second, overlapping loop.
- Controls honor Reduce Transparency; icons carry accessibility labels.

### Fixed
- macOS: the app now plays the stream (`PlayerController` is no longer iOS/visionOS-only; Now Playing, media keys and the play/pause button work; artwork is encoded with ImageIO instead of UIKit). A refresh no longer replaces the player, which would have cut off playback.
- macOS: the show header and "Now Playing" label were hidden and the edges were rough, because a play row's blurred backdrop was not clipped and spilled over its neighbours. The backdrop is now the row's clipped background, list rows have no alternating bands, rows fill the window width, and the expanded details use up to 720 pt of it (340 pt on iOS and visionOS). The DJ comment was drawn twice on macOS; it is drawn once.
- macOS: a Previously row only answered a click in its top half (the tap handler sat on the row's content, inside the scroll view; measured with a click log, taps 31 and 63 pt down fired and 85 pt and deeper did not). The tap handler is now on the finished row, so the whole row toggles, and the expanded details no longer contain a nested scroll view on the Mac, which would also have taken clicks. The overlay-scroller helper no longer takes clicks.
- macOS: the play/pause button sits on the "Now Playing" label line instead of beside the row, so the row keeps the full width. The playlist is a scroll view with the same margins as Now Playing (a Mac `List` added its own insets), and scrollers are overlay-style, so a permanent scrollbar no longer takes a gutter.
- iOS: the play/pause button did not follow the player. `PlayerController` was an `ObservableObject` held by an `@Observable` `ResultRetriever`, so a change to `isPlaying` never reached the view; it is `@Observable` now, with a test that a view reading `isPlaying` is notified.
- Playback resumes after an interruption (a phone call) when the system says it may, and pauses when headphones are unplugged.
- Artwork loading no longer crashes on an unreachable or malformed URL (`try!`), and only contacts
  kexp.org and archive.org over https. Loaded artwork now actually reaches the lock screen.
- Removed `.animation(_, value: 1.0)` modifiers that keyed off a constant and never animated.

### Removed
- The in-repo KEXPPower framework, its XCTest target, the UIKit `KEXPPowerExample` app, the CocoaPods podspec,
  and two dead UI-test targets.
- `beginReceivingRemoteControlEvents()` and a dead macOS block in the app delegate.

### Added
- Links out: Spotify and Bandcamp searches for the playing track, and web links in DJ comments are now tappable (`MusicLinks`).
- `AudioSessionCoordinator` owns the audio session; the app delegate is removed.
- The app and its tests build in Swift 6 language mode.
- Stream quality setting (160 kbps standard, 64 kbps data saver), in Settings on macOS.
- `ArtworkLoader`, `NowPlayingSnapshot`, `NowPlayingPublishing`, and a hosted unit-test target
  (`KEXPPowerAppTests`, Swift Testing).
- Privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`; no tracking, no collected data).
